Privacy Policy

Application: Claim.io (com.rvtechsolutions.claimio)
Last updated: September 17, 2026

🛡️ Summary: Claim.io is an offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data.

1. Introduction

RV Tech Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Claim.io mobile game application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

2. Information Collection and Transmission

The App is designed as a standalone, offline game.

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
  • No Account Creation: No user account or registration is required to play the game.
  • No Remote Data Sharing: The App does not send any telemetry, analytics, or diagnostics over the internet to remote servers.
  • No Advertising Trackers: We do not use advertising identifiers (AAID) or tracking cookies.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimum device permissions necessary for core gameplay functionality:

Permission Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Used strictly on-device to provide haptic feedback during gameplay interactions.

The App does not request internet permissions and does not access external network resources.

4. On-Device Data Storage

The App uses local storage on your device (such as HTML5 localStorage) exclusively to preserve your gameplay progress, high scores, and in-game settings (e.g., sound volume and haptic preferences).

This information remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded or transferred elsewhere. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the App's storage via your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Third-Party Services

Claim.io does not include any third-party SDKs, external analytics libraries, or advertising networks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or AdMob).

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our App is family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever:

  • We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding minors.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in applicable jurisdictions).

7. Security

Because Claim.io operates entirely offline and does not transmit data over the network, your information is not subject to online transmission risks or external server breaches.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
