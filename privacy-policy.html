Privacy Policy

App: Interview Puzzles
Last Updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Interview Puzzles, developed by RV Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd., handles information when you use the application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information that the application stores and how that information is handled.

1. Information We Handle

Interview Puzzles is designed primarily to operate using data stored locally on your device.

Depending on how you use the application, you may enter or create the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your target company
  • Your target role
  • Puzzle progress
  • Completed puzzles and levels
  • Star ratings and bookmarks
  • Other application preferences and progress information
Important: The information listed above is stored locally on your device for application functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers by the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information stored by the application is used to provide the application's features and functionality.

This may include:

  • Saving your interview profile
  • Remembering your puzzle progress
  • Saving completed levels
  • Saving bookmarks and ratings
  • Maintaining your application preferences
  • Providing a personalized experience within the application

We do not use locally stored information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or selling personal information.

3. Data Storage

The application stores supported profile and gameplay information locally on your device.

This local storage allows the application to remember your progress and preferences when you return to the application.

We do not intentionally maintain a remote copy of this locally stored information on our own servers.

4. Internet Permission

The Android application may request Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET).

Internet access may be used for application resources such as loading web content, application assets, fonts, or other resources required for the application to function correctly.

The application does not intentionally use Internet access to transmit your locally stored profile information or gameplay progress to our servers.

5. Analytics and Advertising

Interview Puzzles does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to track users.

The application does not intentionally integrate services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Mixpanel
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or other third parties.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or share your locally stored personal information with third parties.

Because the information described in this policy is stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers, we do not have access to that locally stored information through the application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain locally stored application data only for as long as it remains on your device and is required for the application's functionality.

Your locally stored profile information, puzzle progress, bookmarks, ratings, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's storage/data, or uninstall the application.

How to Delete Your Data

You can remove locally stored application data by using the application's available reset/delete functionality, if provided, or by clearing the application's storage/data through your device settings.

Uninstalling Interview Puzzles will also remove application data stored locally by the application, subject to the normal behavior of your device and operating system.

Since we do not intentionally maintain a server-side copy of this locally stored information, there is no separate server account or server database from which this local data needs to be deleted.

8. Children's Privacy

Interview Puzzles is not designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children through our servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

9. Device Permissions

The application requests only permissions that are necessary for its functionality.

The application does not intentionally access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device information for tracking purposes.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

Because supported profile and gameplay information is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device, operating system, and device access controls.

We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with an appropriate screen lock or other security mechanism.

11. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on standard platform or infrastructure services required for delivering application functionality.

Any third-party service used in a future version of the application that collects or processes personal or sensitive user data will be reviewed and this Privacy Policy will be updated where required.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by Interview Puzzles, please contact us.
