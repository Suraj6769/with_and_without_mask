Privacy Summary: Claim.io is designed as an offline game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal user information with external servers. Gameplay progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

RV Tech Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Claim.io mobile game application (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information associated with your use of the App. This Privacy Policy explains how Claim.io handles information and how locally stored gameplay data is retained and deleted.

2. Information Collection and Transmission

Claim.io is designed as a standalone, offline game. The App does not require an account or registration to use its core gameplay functionality.

No Personal Information Collected

We do not intentionally collect or transmit personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Audio recordings
  • Authentication credentials
  • Advertising identifiers

No Account Creation

Claim.io does not require users to create an account, register, or provide personal information in order to play the game.

No Remote Data Transmission

The App does not intentionally send gameplay information, analytics, telemetry, diagnostics, or personal information to remote servers.

No Advertising Tracking

Claim.io does not intentionally use advertising identifiers or tracking technologies to create user profiles or track users across applications or websites.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimum device permission necessary for its core functionality.

Permission Purpose Data Transmission
android.permission.VIBRATE Used on-device to provide haptic feedback during gameplay interactions. No data is transmitted.

The App does not intentionally use this permission to collect personal information.

Claim.io does not intentionally request Internet permission and is designed to operate without external network access.

4. On-Device Data Storage

Claim.io may use local storage on your device, including technologies such as HTML5 localStorage, to preserve gameplay information.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Gameplay progress
  • High scores
  • Game settings
  • Sound volume preferences
  • Haptic preferences
  • Other gameplay preferences

This information remains on your device and is not intentionally uploaded or transferred to RV Tech Solutions or external servers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data is retained on your device for as long as it is needed to preserve your gameplay progress, scores, and application preferences.

RV Tech Solutions does not intentionally maintain a server-side copy of this locally stored gameplay data.

Therefore, there is no remote retention period for this gameplay information on our servers.

How to Delete Your Data

You can delete locally stored Claim.io data at any time by clearing the App's storage through your Android device settings.

Typical Android path: Settings → Apps → Claim.io → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data

You may also delete locally stored application data by uninstalling Claim.io from your device.

Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the App may permanently remove gameplay progress, high scores, and saved preferences.

Because Claim.io does not intentionally maintain a server-side account or database containing this local gameplay information, no separate server-side deletion request is required for this data.

6. Third-Party Services

Claim.io does not intentionally include third-party analytics libraries, advertising networks, or external tracking services.

The App does not intentionally use services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Meta/Facebook Analytics
  • Unity Ads
  • Mixpanel

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Claim.io is a family-friendly game and does not require users to provide personal information to play.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through remote servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that requires action, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Security

Because Claim.io is designed to operate offline and does not intentionally transmit locally stored gameplay data over the network, there is no remote server database maintained by us containing this gameplay information.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with an appropriate screen lock or other security mechanism.

9. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Gameplay data stored locally on your device is not intentionally transmitted to us or third parties.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to the application, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy for the latest information.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Claim.io's privacy practices, please contact us.
