# Privacy Policy for Pause Challenge

**App Name:** Pause Challenge  
**Package Name:** `com.pause_challenge_game`  
**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  

---

### Summary
Pause Challenge is an offline timing & reflex puzzle game. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device data, or location from our users.**

---

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Personal Information:** None. We do not require any accounts, email addresses, names, or phone numbers.
- **Device & Location Data:** None. We do not track IP addresses, IMEI, MAC address, GPS, or advertising IDs.
- **Financial Information:** None. The game is free and contains no in-app purchases.

### 2. Local Storage & Saved Records
All game progress (levels unlocked, high score, star ratings, and accuracy) is stored **strictly locally on your device** via internal storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

### 3. Permissions Used
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Exclusively used for tactile haptic feedback when pressing the PAUSE button or completing matches. It does not access any personal data.

### 4. Third-Party Services & Ads
- **Zero Third-Party SDKs**: No third-party advertising networks (no AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.).
- **Zero Analytics**: No Google Analytics, Firebase, or tracking tools.

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)
Pause Challenge is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect any information from children or any users.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out to:  
- **Email:** `support@pausechallenge.game`  
- **App:** Pause Challenge (`com.pause_challenge_game`)
