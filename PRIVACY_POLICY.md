# Privacy Policy for Memory Match Challenge

**App Name:** Memory Match Challenge  
**Package Name:** `com.rvtech.memorymatch`  
**Developer:** RV Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  

---

### Summary

Memory Match Challenge is an offline memory and puzzle game.

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device data, or location information from our users.**

The game does not require an account, registration, or submission of personal information.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Personal Information:** None. We do not require or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or user accounts.

- **Device & Location Data:** None. We do not collect or track IP addresses, IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, GPS/location information, advertising IDs, or other device-identifying information.

- **Financial Information:** None. The game does not require payment information and contains no in-app purchases.

---

### 2. Local Storage & Saved Records

Game progress and gameplay records may be stored **strictly locally on your device** using the application's internal storage.

This may include information such as:

- Game progress
- Levels or stages completed
- High scores
- Best performance
- Accuracy or gameplay statistics
- Other locally saved game preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

**RV Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. does not have access to this locally stored game data, and the data is not shared with third parties.**

---

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Memory Match Challenge does not collect or store personal information on our servers, we do not maintain a server-side retention period for user personal data.

Game progress and other locally stored gameplay records remain on your device until you:

- Clear the application's data through your device settings;
- Delete the locally stored game data, where supported by your device; or
- Uninstall the application.

When the application is uninstalled, locally stored application data is generally removed by the Android operating system according to the device's operating-system behavior.

We do not retain copies of this locally stored gameplay information on our servers.

If you have any questions about data retention or deletion, please contact us at:

**Email:** `support@rvtech.com`

---

### 4. Permissions Used

Memory Match Challenge uses only the permissions necessary for the game's functionality.

If the application uses:

- `android.permission.VIBRATE`

this permission is used exclusively for tactile/haptic feedback during gameplay.

The vibration permission does not provide access to personal information, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other sensitive user data.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & Ads

**Zero Third-Party SDKs:** Memory Match Challenge does not use third-party advertising networks or third-party SDKs for collecting user information.

**Zero Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or other analytics/tracking tools to monitor individual users.

**No Advertising:** The game does not use third-party advertising networks such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or similar advertising services.

**No Data Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or share user personal information with third parties.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

Memory Match Challenge is designed to be suitable for users of all ages, including children.

We do not knowingly collect, request, or store personal information from children or any other users.

The game does not require users to create an account, provide an email address, submit personal information, or share their location.

Because the game does not collect personal information, children's personal information is not collected or transmitted by the application.

---

### 7. Data Security

Since Memory Match Challenge does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a database of users' personal information.

Locally stored gameplay data is protected by the security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system and the user's device.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and protecting access to their device.
